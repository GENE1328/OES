October Electrical Services exists to make practical electrical services more accessible to the community — with safety at the centre.

THE PURPOSE

A practical business with a community focus.

The business was registered to provide private electrical services where basic services are needed, while creating opportunity for people who may otherwise be overlooked.

October Electrical Services is based at Graceville, along Sogeri Road, Bomana, Port Moresby. The business profile identifies Bradly Pakurumia as director.

MISSION

Provide private electrical services to the community where basic services are needed to be reached with safety to guarantee clients’ interest as part of infrastructure service delivery.

VISION

Provide safe electrical services as part of infrastructure service delivery and bring electricity to people with safety measures when serving the community.
